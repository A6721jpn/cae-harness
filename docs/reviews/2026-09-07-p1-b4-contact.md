## Implemented contact intent

- `ContactId` provides explicit opaque identity for one contact pair. `ContactIntent` requires distinct part/tool body identities within a source geometry, exact `part_contact_surface` and `tool_contact_surface` roles, and both named selections in one explicitly evidenced `pair_frame`. Body identity is scoped to the pair `(source_geometry_digest, BodyId)`: equal body IDs from different source geometries are not treated as a collision. No body-name inference is used.
- Part/tool assignment evidence targets `contact.part_surface` and `contact.tool_surface`; pair-frame evidence targets `contact.frame`; nested `SelectionRef.role_evidence` remains bound to `selection.role`. No primary/secondary solver assignment is present.
- `Frictionless` is an explicit tagged choice with its own `contact.friction_model` evidence. `CoulombFriction` is a separate tagged choice requiring a finite nonnegative dimensionless `Quantity`, `contact.friction_model` evidence, and `contact.friction_coefficient` evidence. Explicit Coulomb coefficient zero remains distinct from explicit frictionless intent. No default coefficient, penalty, search radius, or solver contact vocabulary exists.
- `AsPlaced` explicitly retains supplied placement and requires `contact.arrangement` evidence. `SpecifiedGap` requires signed length `gap`, normalized `UnitDirection`, and arrangement/gap/direction evidence. The direction is documented from part toward tool: positive gap requests separation, zero touching, and negative initial overlap. Its frame must equal the evidenced pair frame. No gap measurement or body movement occurs.
- Canonical bytes normalize quantities to SI and compose the existing `SelectionRef.to_bytes()` semantic face-set normalization through canonical JSON projection. Nested selection, friction, arrangement, and quantity range failures are forced at construction. The intent is immutable, rejects unknown constructor fields through strict dataclass signatures, and intentionally has no `from_dict` boundary.
